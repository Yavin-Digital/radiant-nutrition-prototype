Section 06

Usage Rules

Logo Sizing

  • Minimum 160px wide in navigation
  • Minimum 240px wide in hero sections and brand documents
  • Always maintain original aspect ratio

Logo Usage — Do

  • Use the original logo on white or cream backgrounds
  • Apply brightness(0) invert(1) filter on dark backgrounds
  • Ensure clear space equal to the logo height on all sides

Logo Usage — Do Not

  • Never stretch or distort the logo
  • Never place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Never alter logo colors or apply unapproved filters

Copy & Design Rules

  • No emojis in professional copy or print materials
  • Always maintain WCAG AA color contrast minimums
  • Do not pair Cormorant Garamond at small sizes (below 14px)
  • Do not use more than two typeface weights per layout section
