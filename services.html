Our Nutrition Services

Service

Weight Management

Achieving a healthy weight isn't about willpower — it's about having the right plan, the right support, and the right mindset. Our registered dietitians take a sustainable, non-diet approach to weight management that works with your lifestyle, not against it.

We use InBody scanning technology to give you a precise picture of your body composition so we can track real progress — not just the number on the scale.

  • Personalized caloric and macronutrient targets
  • InBody scan for body composition analysis
  • Behavior change and sustainable habit-building
  • GLP-1 / peptide-paired nutrition support available
  • Covered by most major insurance plans

Sports Nutrition

Whether you're a high school student-athlete, a weekend warrior, or a seasoned competitor, what you eat directly impacts how you perform and recover. Our sports nutrition program is built around your sport, your training schedule, and your goals.

Dietitian Sabrina Monaco specializes in sports performance nutrition and has a passion for helping athletes of all levels reach their potential through strategic fueling.

  • Pre- and post-workout fueling strategies
  • Hydration and electrolyte planning
  • Competition-day and travel nutrition
  • Body composition optimization without restrictive dieting
  • Available for student-athletes and families

Diabetes Management

Managing diabetes — Type 1 or Type 2 — requires a nuanced, individualized approach to nutrition. Our dietitians provide Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) specifically designed to help you achieve better blood sugar control, reduce A1C levels, and improve your quality of life.

We work in partnership with your physician and endocrinologist to ensure your nutrition plan aligns with your overall medical care.

  • Personalized meal planning for blood sugar control
  • Carbohydrate counting and glycemic index education
  • Type 1 & Type 2 diabetes support
  • Pre-diabetes prevention and intervention
  • Covered under most insurance plans as Medical Nutrition Therapy
Book a Diabetes Visit

Pediatric Nutrition

Children and teens have unique nutritional needs — and getting it right early can set the foundation for a lifetime of health. Our dietitians are experienced in working with pediatric clients across all ages, from toddlers to teenagers.

Dietitian Jenny Brewton is a mother of three teenagers (including two student-athletes) and brings both professional expertise and real-life empathy to every family she works with.

  • Picky eating and mealtime struggles
  • Pediatric weight management (certified)
  • Teen and student-athlete fueling
  • Family-centered nutrition education
  • Ages 2 and up — all ages welcome

Heart Health Nutrition

Cardiovascular disease is one of the most diet-responsive chronic conditions. Through evidence-based nutritional strategies, we help clients reduce their risk factors — and in many cases, see meaningful improvements in cholesterol, blood pressure, and triglyceride levels.

Our dietitians collaborate closely with cardiologists and primary care providers to develop safe, effective nutrition plans as part of your broader cardiac care.

  • High cholesterol & triglyceride management
  • High blood pressure (hypertension) nutrition
  • Heart disease risk reduction
  • DASH diet and Mediterranean diet guidance
  • Post-cardiac event nutrition support
Book a Heart Health Visit
