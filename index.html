About Us

Going Beyond the Basics of Healthy Eating

At Radiant Nutrition, we go beyond the basics of healthy eating. Our mission is to provide expert nutrition guidance while empowering our clients with the knowledge and support they need to make lasting changes.


Our registered dietitians specialize in personalized nutrition for weight management, sports performance, family health, and overall wellness. We utilize evidence-based strategies, practical tools, and innovative technology — such as InBody scans — to ensure you receive the best possible care.


Education is at the heart of what we do. We take the time to help you understand your body's needs, how nutrition impacts your health, and how to implement realistic, sustainable habits tailored to your unique goals.


  • Evidence-based, personalized nutrition plans
  • InBody scanning technology for precision care
  • In-network with most major insurance providers
  • In-person & telehealth appointments available

Our Philosophy

More Than What's on Your Plate

At Radiant Nutrition, we believe that nutrition is more than just what's on your plate — it's about fueling your body, mind, and lifestyle. Our team of experienced dietitians combines evidence-based nutrition strategies with a personalized, whole-person approach to health. We are committed to helping you build sustainable habits that support long-term wellness, whether you're focused on performance, weight management, or overall vitality.

Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How often do I need to follow up?
Follow-up frequency depends on your goals and comfort level. Your dietitian will work with you to decide how often you'd benefit from check-ins — whether it's weekly, biweekly, or monthly — to ensure accountability and ongoing support.
What is your overall philosophy to nutrition?
We believe in a personalized, client-centered approach. Rather than a one-size-fits-all program, we customize our plans to your unique lifestyle, health needs, and preferences. Our goal is to empower you with sustainable habits for long-term success.
Which dietitian should I choose?
Our team of registered dietitians each bring unique expertise — from weight management to sports nutrition, diabetes care, and more. Visit our Meet Our Dietitians page for bios on each team member and find the perfect fit for your needs.
Do you see pediatric clients or student athletes?
Yes! Our dietitians work with clients of all ages, including pediatric patients, student athletes, and adults of all activity levels. Whether managing a chronic condition or striving for peak sports performance, we tailor recommendations to meet your specific goals.
How can I get more information?
Feel free to call our office at (940) 464-9800 or email us at admin@radiantnutrition.com. We're here to answer any questions and help you take the next step in your health journey.
Where is Radiant Nutrition located?
We are located at 306 Hwy 377, Suite F, Argyle, TX 76226 — just after Point Bank on Hwy 377. We are in Argyle, not Roanoke.
Do you accept insurance?
Yes! We are in-network with several major insurance companies. Many plans cover nutrition counseling at little or no out-of-pocket cost. Once you complete our New Client Form, we will help verify your insurance benefits before you get started. Note: We do not work directly with health share programs at this time.
How do I schedule an appointment?
You can schedule by filling out our New Client Form on the website or by contacting our office at (940) 464-9800. Once we have your information, we'll reach out to confirm availability and get you set up with a registered dietitian.
Do you offer telehealth appointments?
Absolutely! We offer secure video appointments that allow you to meet with a dietitian from the comfort of your home or office. Telehealth visits are covered by most insurance plans the same way in-person visits are.
What can I expect at my first appointment?
Your initial visit is typically two hours. During this time, we take a deep dive into your health history, dietary habits, goals, and any prior challenges. You and your dietitian will collaborate on a completely customized plan, and you'll leave with clear next steps to start making progress immediately.
