Section 5

Local SEO Dominance Plan

Google Business Profile Optimization Checklist

  • Claim and verify the listing
  • Add primary and secondary categories
  • Write a keyword-rich business description
  • Upload 20+ high-quality photos
  • Add office interior and exterior photos
  • Add team/headshot photos
  • List all services with descriptions
  • Set accurate business hours
  • Add holiday hours
  • Enable appointment booking link
  • Add telehealth service area
  • List accepted insurance plans
  • Add Q&A section with common questions
  • Post weekly GBP updates
  • Respond to all reviews within 48 hours
  • Enable messaging feature
  • Add products/services with pricing where applicable
  • Ensure NAP matches all directory listings
  • Add website and social links
  • Monitor and resolve duplicate listings

Review Generation Strategy

Goal: 50 Google reviews within 6 months.

  • Send follow-up email after every session with a direct Google review link
  • Place a QR code card in the office directing clients to leave a review
  • Include a review request in the post-appointment SMS or email
  • Thank every reviewer publicly with a personalized response
  • Track monthly review count against the 50-review goal

Local Schema and "Near Me" Optimization

Local schema markup (LocalBusiness, MedicalBusiness) is already implemented across all pages. Maintain and expand as new pages are added.

"Near Me" Keyword Optimization Guide
  • Include "near Argyle," "near Denton," and "North Texas" in page copy naturally
  • Create a dedicated service area page listing all served cities
  • Use city-specific landing pages for Argyle, Denton, Flower Mound, and Southlake
  • Embed a Google Map on the Contact page
  • Add geo-coordinates to LocalBusiness schema
