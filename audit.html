Section 02

Website Audit

Review of radiantnutrition.com hosted on GoHighLevel, covering design, functionality, and conversion elements.

Insurance acceptance is prominently mentioned

Clients searching "dietitian near me accepts insurance" find this reassuring. Keep it in the hero, FAQ, and every CTA section.

Telehealth offering is clearly communicated

Telehealth expands your reach far beyond Argyle. Mention it in the hero, services, and FAQ on every page.

Authentic client testimonials are present

Real Google review quotes build trust. Adding photos and a total review count would increase credibility further.

No team bios or dietitian photos

Clients want to know who they're working with before booking. A "Meet the Team" section with headshots and credentials is a high-converting addition.

Only one CTA path (Insurance Verification Form)

Many visitors aren't ready for a full form. Add a secondary CTA — "Call us" or "Book a free 15-min discovery call" — to capture warm leads earlier.

No blog or educational content

Blog posts are the fastest path to free organic Google traffic. Even 4–6 posts per year targeting local health searches makes a measurable difference.

No Google Business Profile link or reviews widget

A GBP reviews widget or direct link reinforces local SEO signals and helps fence-sitters convert.

Slow mobile load speed (GHL funnel overhead)

Minimize custom code blocks, compress images, and avoid loading unused scripts. Test monthly at PageSpeed Insights.

Section 04

SEO Opportunities

Local SEO is the highest-ROI channel for a brick-and-mortar dietitian practice. These are the most impactful actions ranked by effort vs. reward.

Google Business Profile not fully optimized

Add all services, hours, photos, and a booking link. Post weekly GBP updates. This is the single highest-impact free SEO action available.

Page titles and meta descriptions need rewriting

Current title: generic. Target: "Registered Dietitian in Argyle, TX | Insurance Accepted | Radiant Nutrition." Each GHL page needs a unique, keyword-rich title and 155-character meta description.

No local keyword targeting in body copy

Include phrases like "dietitian Argyle TX," "nutrition counseling Denton County," and "telehealth dietitian Texas" naturally in headings and body text.

Zero backlinks from local sources

Get listed on local directories (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Psychology Today, Vitals, local chamber). Each listing = a backlink + new discovery channel.

Address and phone are present on the site

NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency is a local ranking factor. Make sure the exact same format appears on your GBP, website, and every directory listing.

Priority keyword targets: "dietitian Argyle TX" · "registered dietitian near me" · "nutrition counseling accepts insurance" · "sports nutrition Denton County" · "telehealth dietitian Texas" · "pediatric dietitian Argyle"

Section 05

Content Gaps

Pages and content types that competing practices offer — and that clients actively search for — that Radiant Nutrition is currently missing.

Meet the Team page

Individual dietitian bios, credentials, specialties, and a personal photo. This is the most-visited page on most healthcare sites.

Blog / Resource Center

Even 1 post per month on topics like "What to eat if you have high cholesterol" drives compounding organic traffic for years.

Insurance Accepted page

A dedicated page listing every accepted insurer with a "verify my benefits" CTA is a high-converting asset for cost-conscious clients.

Before/After or Results page

With client permission, showcase transformation stories with specific outcomes (lbs lost, A1C reduced, energy gained).

New Client Resources / What to Expect

A simple "How It Works" page or section reduces anxiety for first-time nutrition clients and pre-qualifies them before they call.

Email newsletter opt-in

Capture emails from visitors who aren't ready to book. A monthly "Nutrition Tip" email keeps Radiant Nutrition top of mind until they're ready.

Section 06

User Experience (UX)

No sticky "Book Now" button on mobile

Mobile users scroll past the CTA. A fixed bottom bar with a phone/book button on mobile would dramatically increase conversions.

Phone number is not click-to-call styled

All phone numbers on mobile should be wrapped in href="tel:" links and visually styled as buttons.

Insurance form is the first commitment asked

Move the insurance form deeper in the funnel. Lead with a lower-friction CTA (discovery call or phone call) so you don't lose warmer-but-hesitant visitors.

FAQ section reduces pre-call anxiety

The current FAQ addresses the most common objections (insurance, location, telehealth, first visit). Keep and expand this section.

Section 07

Social Media Strategy

Social media for a local healthcare practice is not about going viral — it's about staying visible, building trust, and giving people a reason to think of you when they're ready to take action.

InstagramPrimary Platform

Your audience skews female, 28–55, health-conscious. Instagram's visual format is perfect for before/after results, recipe reels, and relatable nutrition content. Goal: 3–4 posts/week.

FacebookSecondary Platform

Ideal for sharing to local Argyle / Denton County community groups, running targeted local ads, and longer-form content. Goal: 2–3 posts/week, cross-posted from Instagram.

GHL Integration Tip: Use GHL's Social Planner to schedule posts for both Instagram and Facebook from one dashboard. Set up a recurring posting template so content goes out even during busy weeks.

Section 08

Content Pillars

Every post you create should belong to one of these five pillars. This keeps your feed varied, purposeful, and on-brand — without having to think from scratch every time.

Educate

Nutrition tips, myth-busting, science-backed facts. Positions your team as the local experts.

~30% of posts
Inspire

Client wins, motivational quotes, transformation stories. Emotional connection and social proof.

~25% of posts
Recipes

Quick, healthy recipes your audience can actually make. Highly shareable and saveable on Instagram.

~20% of posts
Behind the Scenes

Meet the team, office moments, a day in the life. Builds trust and makes your brand feel human.

~15% of posts
Promote

Service spotlights, booking CTAs, insurance reminders, telehealth promotions. Direct ask for the appointment.

~10% of posts
