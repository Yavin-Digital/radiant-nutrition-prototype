Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this program right for me if I’m not currently on any peptide protocol?

Absolutely. This program is designed for anyone who wants to understand what peptides are and what the current research says — whether you are considering a protocol, currently working with a provider, or simply curious. The goal is education, not a specific treatment plan.

Will this program tell me what peptides to take or what dose to use?

No. This is an educational program only. Nothing in this program constitutes medical advice, a treatment protocol, a prescription, or a dosing recommendation. All decisions about whether to use any peptide or protocol must be made with a licensed, qualified healthcare provider who knows your individual health history.

Who teaches this program and what are their credentials?

This program is created and taught by Janna Weaver, a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist (RDN) with extensive clinical experience in medical nutrition therapy. Janna is the founder of Radiant Nutrition in Argyle, Texas. She is not a physician and does not prescribe, diagnose, treat, or cure any medical condition. Her expertise is in nutrition — specifically how nutrition intersects with and supports various health protocols.

Are peptides FDA-approved?

Some peptides (such as certain GLP-1 receptor agonists) are FDA-approved for specific medical indications. Many others discussed in this program are investigational, meaning they are the subject of ongoing research but have not received FDA approval for the uses discussed here. This program educates you on what the research currently shows and does not advocate for any off-label use without physician oversight.

How is this different from what I can read online?

Most online content about peptides is either highly technical research papers or anecdotal claims from unqualified sources. This program sits in the middle — credentialed, evidence-based, and designed for people without a medical background who want to understand the science without needing a PhD to follow along. Every claim is tied to research, and the limitations of that research are clearly explained.

Does taking this program make me a patient of Radiant Nutrition?

No. Enrollment in this program does not create a patient-provider relationship between you and Janna Weaver or Radiant Nutrition. If you are interested in personalized nutrition counseling, you are welcome to schedule a clinical appointment separately through radiantnutrition.com. This program is a standalone educational product.

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with the program, contact us within 30 days of purchase and we will issue a full refund.
