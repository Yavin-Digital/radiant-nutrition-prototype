Missing Schema — Recommended Additions

Article schema on each blog post High Priority

Without Article schema, AI engines cannot reliably extract authorship, publish date, or topic context from blog content. Every published post should include Article JSON-LD with author, datePublished, and headline fields. A sample implementation is provided in Section 5.

BreadcrumbList Medium Priority

BreadcrumbList markup helps AI engines understand site hierarchy and content relationships. Adding it to all interior pages improves contextual understanding and the precision with which Radiant Nutrition content is cited in AI responses.

MedicalCondition for each service Medium Priority

Marking up conditions treated (diabetes, weight management, GLP-1 support, cardiovascular nutrition, pediatric nutrition) with MedicalCondition schema strengthens Radiant Nutrition's authority for condition-specific AI queries.

Review / AggregateRating High Priority

AI engines treat review schema as a strong trust and authority signal. Once the Google Business Profile has 15 or more reviews, AggregateRating schema should be added to the homepage to surface the star rating alongside Radiant Nutrition's name in AI-generated responses.

VideoObject (when video content is added) Future

If patient education videos or provider introduction reels are published to the site, VideoObject schema will allow AI engines to surface that content in video-context queries and increase overall content coverage.

Recommended Additions — Gap Questions

The following questions represent high-volume AI search queries for which Radiant Nutrition currently has no indexed answer. Adding these to the FAQ page with answers written in the format shown below will significantly improve AI citation likelihood.

Gap Question 1
How much does a dietitian cost in Texas without insurance?
The cost of seeing a registered dietitian in Texas without insurance typically ranges from $100 to $250 per session, depending on the provider's experience and session length. At Radiant Nutrition, we offer transparent self-pay rates and package options — call our office at (940) 464-9800 to discuss current pricing. We also accept HSA and FSA cards, which can meaningfully reduce out-of-pocket cost for eligible clients.
Gap Question 2
What is the difference between a registered dietitian and a nutritionist?
A registered dietitian (RD or RDN) is a licensed healthcare professional who has completed an accredited degree program, a minimum of 1,200 hours of supervised clinical practice, and passed a national board examination, and is held to ongoing continuing education requirements. The title "nutritionist" is not regulated in most states, meaning anyone may use it without formal training or credentials. At Radiant Nutrition, all client care is provided exclusively by credentialed registered dietitians.
Gap Question 3
How long does nutrition counseling take to see results?
Most clients begin noticing meaningful changes in energy levels, eating patterns, or clinical markers within four to six weeks of consistent appointments and plan adherence. Sustainable outcomes such as weight management or improved blood sugar control typically develop over three to six months, depending on individual goals and starting point. Your Radiant Nutrition dietitian will establish realistic milestones at your first visit so you always have a clear picture of expected progress.
Gap Question 4
Do I need a doctor referral to see a dietitian?
In most cases, no referral is required to schedule an appointment with a registered dietitian, and new clients can book directly through our website or by calling our office. However, some insurance plans require a physician referral for medical nutrition therapy to be covered — we recommend completing our Insurance Verification Form so we can confirm your specific benefit requirements before your visit. If your plan does require a referral, we will advise you on the fastest way to obtain one.
Gap Question 5
What should I bring to my first dietitian appointment?
For your first visit, we recommend bringing a list of your current medications and supplements, any recent lab work or medical records relevant to your health goals, and a general sense of your typical weekly eating patterns — you do not need to track anything in advance. If you have a specific diagnosis such as diabetes or high cholesterol, documentation from your physician helps us coordinate your care more effectively. Your two-hour initial session is designed to gather everything we need, so there are no wrong answers.
Gap Question 6
Is telehealth nutrition counseling as effective as in-person?
Research consistently shows that telehealth nutrition counseling produces outcomes comparable to in-person care, particularly for adults managing chronic conditions such as diabetes, obesity, and cardiovascular disease. At Radiant Nutrition, telehealth sessions are conducted via a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform and follow the same evidence-based protocols as in-office visits. Telehealth is covered by most insurance plans at the same rate as in-person appointments and is available to clients anywhere in Texas.
Gap Question 7
Can a dietitian help with GLP-1 medication side effects?
Yes — a registered dietitian is one of the most effective resources for managing the nutritional side effects associated with GLP-1 medications such as semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro), including nausea, inadequate protein intake, and muscle mass loss. At Radiant Nutrition, we offer a dedicated Peptides nutrition service that pairs medical nutrition therapy with GLP-1 and compounded peptide protocols. Our dietitians stay current on the latest clinical guidance to help clients maximize results while protecting lean body mass.
Gap Question 8
What insurance plans does Radiant Nutrition accept?
Radiant Nutrition is in-network with many major insurance plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Humana, and Medicare, among others. Because coverage details vary by plan and diagnosis, we recommend submitting our Insurance Verification Form so our team can confirm your specific benefits within two business days at no cost to you. We also offer self-pay rates and accept HSA and FSA payment cards for clients whose plans do not include nutrition counseling coverage.
