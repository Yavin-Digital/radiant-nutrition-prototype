Janna Weaver
RDN, LD
Founder & Owner · Registered Dietitian Nutritionist

I'm Janna, a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist, founder of Radiant Nutrition, and someone who truly understands the struggle of trying to "get it right" when it comes to health and nutrition. With over 20 years of experience, I've built this practice on the belief that nutrition should be realistic, sustainable, and empowering. I specialize in weight loss management, adult and family nutrition, and corporate wellness, combining evidence-based strategies with a compassionate, no-judgment approach. I hold advanced certifications in Adult and Pediatric Weight Management through the Academy of Nutrition & Dietetics and have been honored as a Best of Denton County winner for the past two years, as well as a DFW Mom-Approved provider.

Jenny Brewton
RDN, LD
Registered Dietitian Nutritionist

I'm Jenny, a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist with a heart for helping people feel heard, understood, and empowered in their health journey. My love for nutrition started in high school, and that passion led me to UT Southwestern where I earned my Bachelor of Science in Clinical Nutrition, and then to Parkland's Neonatal ICU.
