Section 02

Key Strengths

Modern, Conversion-Optimized Website
The website includes all essential pages — services, team bios, insurance information, and clear calls to action. Site architecture supports both user experience and search engine indexing, scoring among the top assets in the digital profile.
Insurance-Friendly Positioning
Acceptance of insurance coverage is a major competitive differentiator in the Denton County market. Few competing practices communicate this clearly online. Radiant Nutrition's positioning on this point is a meaningful driver of patient acquisition.
Credentialed, Experienced Team
Three Registered Dietitian Nutritionists with documented credentials establishes deep clinical authority. This expertise is reflected across the website and social profiles, creating a foundation of trust that many competitors lack entirely.
Section 03

Critical Gaps

No Video Presence
YouTube and TikTok score zero — no active channels exist. Nutrition, dietetics, and wellness content rank among the most-watched health categories online. Video is where trust is built at scale, and the absence here represents the largest untapped opportunity in the profile.
Google Business Profile Underperforming
The Google Business Profile is present but lacks sufficient reviews and regular post activity. Prospective patients rely heavily on review volume and recency before booking a first appointment — this gap directly affects patient acquisition and local search ranking.
Email Marketing Not Active
Email consistently delivers the highest ROI of any digital marketing channel in healthcare — yet no active program exists. A monthly newsletter would nurture existing patients, generate referrals, and reactivate lapsed relationships with minimal ongoing effort.

Why Act Now

Competitor analysis of the Denton County market identifies two nutrition practices that have accelerated their digital investment over the past 12 months — increasing Google Business review counts, launching video content, and expanding social posting frequency. Acting now positions Radiant Nutrition as the recognized digital authority for dietitian services in Denton County — before competitors close the gap.
